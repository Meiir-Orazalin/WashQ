# Product vision

WashQueue KZ reduces uncertainty around finding and visiting a car wash. It will
combine a verified marketplace, scheduled bookings, and an approximate live
queue while giving operators one place to run branch operations.

## Outcomes

- Customers can choose a suitable verified car wash with clear services, prices,
  availability, and queue context.
- Businesses can coordinate bookings, walk-ins, wash boxes, employees, and
  service progress without fragmented tools.
- Platform administrators can protect marketplace quality through verification,
  moderation, audit history, and platform-level reporting.

Queue information is approximate and must be presented as such. The platform
does not promise an exact service-start time when real-world operations change.
