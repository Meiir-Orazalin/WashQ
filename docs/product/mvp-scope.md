# MVP scope

The MVP is expected to cover Versions 1 through 6 of the roadmap:

- customer identity, profile, and vehicles;
- business organizations, branches, hours, boxes, employees, services, and prices;
- public marketplace discovery, branch details, filters, sorting, favorites,
  and location data;
- booking availability, lifecycle, conflict prevention, and history;
- walk-in and booked-customer queue operations with wash-box assignment and
  concurrency protection;
- verified reviews, business replies, complaints, and moderation.

Versions are delivered incrementally. A module is not created until its first
use case is implemented and tested.
