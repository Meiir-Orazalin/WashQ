# Users

## Customers

Drivers who discover nearby car washes, compare verified services and prices,
manage vehicles, book a time, join or track a queue, save favorites, and review
completed orders.

## Car wash businesses

Owners, managers, and employees who operate organizations and branches. Their
work includes opening hours, wash boxes, service catalogs, pricing, bookings,
walk-ins, vehicle status, operational analytics, and review responses.

## Platform administrators

Internal operators who verify organizations, manage marketplace participants,
moderate reviews and complaints, inspect audit history, and monitor aggregate
platform health.

These roles describe future product authorization boundaries. Version 0 has no
identity, authentication, or authorization implementation.
